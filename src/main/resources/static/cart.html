🛒 Cart Order Service

Cart Management Dashboard

Display products first, select items, add to cart, view orders and remove products.

👤

Admin Sign-in

POST

Not signed in. Sign in with your own admin account (what you can do depends on your role), or use the shared Service Key below.

🔑

Service Credentials

REQUIRED

Place Order, All Orders, Ship Order, Deliver Order, Add Coupon, Add To Wishlist, Remove From Wishlist and Delete Product require the same X-Service-Key value as this service's .env. Place Order also requires the buyer's own PhonepayService session token, sent exactly as PhonepayService expects it (e.g. Bearer <token>) - that is who gets charged. Both are remembered in this browser only. When you are signed in above as a named admin, that sign-in is used instead of the Service Key.

📥

Needs Attention

GET

Everything waiting on someone: support requests, unanswered questions, reported reviews, low stock, UPI orders stuck past their payment deadline and notification emails that never went out. "Open" takes you to that card and loads it. Requires the Service Key above.

💰

Refunds To Settle

GET

Refunds the system could not make by itself (a checkout failed after the buyer was charged and the automatic refund did not go through). Refund the buyer by hand, then mark it resolved with a note. Requires the Service Key above.

↩

Return Requests

GET

Customers asking to return a delivered order (only when RETURNS_REQUIRE_APPROVAL is on). Approve to let them complete the return themselves, or reject with a note. Requires the Service Key above.

🚚

Return Pickups

GET

Returns booked by customers while RETURNS_PICKUP is on. Mark them picked up, then received - the refund is paid on "received". Requires the Service Key above.

Exchange Requests

GET

Customers swapping a delivered item for another product at the same price or lower. Approve sets the replacement aside in stock; "Received" puts the old item back in stock. Requires the Service Key above.

Failed Delivery

POST

Record that a delivery attempt on a shipped order failed - the customer is told which attempt it was. Once you have recorded at least one, "Send back to stock" cancels the order, restocks it and refunds it. Requires the Service Key above.

Packing Slips & Pick List

GET

Print one slip per order (address, delivery window, gift message, cash to collect) plus a combined pick list for the whole batch. Leave the order IDs empty for every placed order, or list some separated by commas. Requires the Service Key above.

🎟

Coupon Performance

GET

How each coupon did: orders, discount given and revenue from the orders that still stand, plus how many were cancelled or returned. Requires the Service Key above.

Revenue Over Time

GET

Revenue per day or week, net of refunds. Orders count in the period they were placed; cancelled and unpaid UPI orders are left out. Requires the Service Key above.

📊

Sales Breakdown

GET

What sold, by product or by category: units, orders, revenue (net of refunds, coupons included) and share of the total. Orders count on the day they were placed; cancelled, returned and unpaid UPI orders are left out. Click a column heading to sort. Needs a manager or owner (or the Service Key above).

🧾

GST Report

GET

Tax invoices and the credit notes that reverse some of them, by month, state (place of supply) and GST rate, with the net - what the month's GST filing needs. Invoices count at the quantities they were issued for; later cancellations and returns show as credit notes. Needs a manager or owner (or the Service Key above).

Gift Cards

POST

Mint one-time codes worth a fixed amount. A customer enters one under My account to add its value to their store credit. The codes are shown once, right after minting - copy them then (only a hash is kept). Owners only (or the Service Key above).

👤

Customer Lookup

GET

Everything about one customer: sign-in email, orders and net spend, loyalty tier, saved addresses. Search by phone number or by the email they signed in with. Requires the Service Key above.

👥

Admin Accounts

POST

The people who can sign in above. SUPPORT looks customers and orders up and answers questions and support requests. MANAGER also ships, delivers, cancels, refunds and runs coupons, pincodes and analytics. OWNER also manages these accounts, reads the audit log and adjusts store credit and loyalty points. Needs an owner account (or the Service Key above). Once an owner exists you can switch the Service Key off for browsers entirely (server setting ADMIN_REQUIRE_NAMED_LOGIN=true), so every change is made by a named person.

📝

Admin Audit Log

GET

Every change made with the Service Key or an admin account (shipping, coupons, pincodes, answers, ...): who, when, what, and whether it succeeded - including attempts a role was not allowed to make (403). Only the endpoint is recorded, never the data. Owners only (or the Service Key above).

Returns & Cancellations

💵

Cash Collection

GET

Cash-on-delivery money: collected per day (from when you pressed Mark Paid), still owed, and delivered orders whose cash is overdue. Uses your browser's time zone. Requires the Service Key above.

📅

Today

GET

Today's orders and revenue, plus what needs doing now: orders waiting too long to ship, delivered cash orders not yet marked paid, UPI payments still pending and unanswered customer questions. Uses your browser's time zone. Requires the Service Key above.

🔍

Missed Searches

GET

Words shoppers typed into the storefront search that matched no product, most frequent first. Only the word is kept - no phone number or account. Good input for what to stock next. Requires the Service Key above.

📦

Inventory Value

What the stock on the shelves is worth at today's selling prices (GST included), in total and per category. Requires the Service Key above.

Slow Movers

GET

Products with stock on the shelf that have not sold in the last N days, biggest tied-up value first. Cancelled and returned units do not count as sales. Requires the Service Key above.

🛒

Reorder Suggestions

GET

Products that would run out within the cover period at the pace of recent sales, with how many units to add so they last that long. Based on past sales only (cancelled and returned units do not count) - not a forecast. Requires the Service Key above.

🛡️

Review Moderation

GET / PUT

Reviews customers have reported, newest queue first. Hiding a review removes it (and its rating) from the storefront; Undo restores it. Requires the Service Key above.

📷

Tidy Review Photos

POST

Photos stay on disk after their review is deleted, or if a customer uploads one and never posts it. Preview lists the files no review uses (hidden reviews still count as using theirs); Delete removes them for good. Photos uploaded in the last hour are never touched. If ProductService cannot be reached nothing is deleted. Requires the Service Key above.

🏷

Price-Match Requests

GET / PUT

Customers who found a product cheaper elsewhere, oldest first, with their price and link. Approving or declining sends your note back to the customer on the product page; it does not change any price (start a sale for that). Requires the Service Key above.

❓

Customer Questions

GET / PUT / DELETE

Questions customers have asked on product pages, oldest first. An answer appears on the product page right away; unanswered questions stay hidden. Requires the Service Key above.

💬

Support Requests

GET / POST

"Problem with this order" requests from customers - waiting longest first. Each reply is emailed to the customer. Use the cancel / return / per-item refund tools for the money side, then resolve the request. Requires the Service Key above.
